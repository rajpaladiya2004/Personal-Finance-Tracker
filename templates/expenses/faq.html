FAQ

Frequently asked questions about using Personal Finance Tracker.

How do I add a transaction?

Go to the dashboard or Transactions page and use the Add Transaction button.

How do I see my notifications?

Open the Notifications page from the top navigation to view recent income and expense alerts.

Can I edit or delete a transaction?

Yes. Open the transaction list, then use the edit or delete actions beside the entry.

What should I do if I forget my password?

Use the Change Password option from your account menu after logging in.
